## PROMPT START

Make a 5:15–5:40 landscape (16:9) explainer video titled "Montis Coaching 101 — Tools".

What it is:
Create a clear, human video that shows what a coach does in the Montis App once athletes are connected: see who needs attention, understand why, act, and tell the athlete.
The story follows one Monday morning with:
- Anna Weber, an endurance coach who runs Anna Weber Coaching.
- Her five athletes:
- Sofia Lind, a runner who is strained and needs recovery.
- Lena Brandt, a cyclist in a build block, 27 days from the Gran Fondo Wachau.
- Marco Rossi, tapering for the Alpine Marathon in 6 days.
- Tom Berger, in a stable base block.
- Sam Carter, who has no recent data yet.
This is the second coaching video. The first, Montis Coaching 101 — Setup, shows how Anna invited them. Montis 101 explains the five-layer Intelligence Stack behind every number shown here.
The story in one sentence: Anna opens the Coach Cockpit, sees Sofia and Lena need her first, checks why, sends Lena her weekly report and a session analysis, and plans a change that only happens when she presses Confirm.
Primary message: Montis shows the coach who needs attention and why; the AI explains and drafts, the coach decides

Presenter: one presenter/avatar, conversational rather than technical. Use the same presenter as Montis 101 and the Setup video. Show Anna as an illustrated or lightweight cinematic character; the athletes appear only as their cards and screens. Use the real screens for every step; animate the pointer, highlight and zoom.
Tone: warm, practical, coach-first.

Style: Montis — a calm, clean product explainer on dark backgrounds.
Colours: Night #071310 (backgrounds), Deep green #0c201b (panels), Montis green #19b77a (highlights and buttons), Aqua #55d7c0 (accents), Paper #f4f6f0 and Ink #0b1816 (light cards and their text).
Fonts: Manrope for titles, DM Sans for body text.
Logo: use the attached icon-512.png (the Montis mountain logo) on the opening and closing title cards, and small in a corner during the video.
Screens: the attached PNGs are real Montis app screens with made-up example people and data. Show each one large in a clean rounded frame, with gentle zoom or pan and simple highlight callouts on what the voiceover mentions. Never redraw, crop out or change what the screens show.
Motion: real Montis App screens (linked in each scene), with pointer, highlight and zoom; simple fades between scenes; captions on.

Script rules: follow the scenes in order. Each VO line is exactly what the presenter says, word for word. On-screen text appears exactly as written.
Keep it human:
The whole video answers four plain questions:
1. Who needs me first?
2. Why?
3. What should I tell them?
4. What should change — and who decides?
Acronyms (ADE, TSB, CTL, HRV) can appear on screen, but the presenter translates them.
Guardrails:
Do not say or imply that:
- the AI calculates the cockpit, the decision or any metric;
- the AI changes a calendar without the coach pressing Confirm;
- emails go to any address the coach types;
- the cockpit shows every athlete (it shows up to 25);
- athletes can see each other;
- the example people or AI answers are real.
Use these facts instead:
- every number comes from the Montis coaching engine, from the athlete's Intervals.icu data;
- the AI explains, drafts and answers questions; the coach decides;
- changes to an athlete's calendar or chat need Confirm first;
- emails go to the coach's own or a coached athlete's Intervals.icu email; the workflow report copies the coach in;
- Intervals.icu stays the source of truth.
